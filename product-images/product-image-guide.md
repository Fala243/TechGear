**Shared style for every prompt:**

> Premium studio product photography, single product centered with space around it, dark navy background (#0b1222), subtle electric-blue rim lighting (#3b82f6), soft reflection on a glossy surface, photorealistic, no text, no logos, no people, landscape 4:3, 1600×1200.

### Tips

- Use the **4:3 landscape** shape (e.g. 1600×1200). The site shows images with `object-fit: contain`, so other shapes still work but get navy space around them.
- Keep each file under about 300 KB (export as JPG at about 80% quality) so pages load quickly.
- Only use images you have the right to use, and that don't include real brand logos.
